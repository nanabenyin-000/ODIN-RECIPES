TAGLIATELLE-ALLA-BOLOGNA



DESCRIPTION

Tagliatelle alla Bolognese is a classic Italian pasta dish originating from the city of Bologna.
It features tender ribbons of tagliatelle pasta coated in a rich, slow-cooked meat sauce made with ground beef,
tomatoes, onions, carrots, celery, and aromatic herbs.

The sauce is simmered for hours, allowing the flavors to blend together and create a hearty,
comforting meal.Known for its deep flavor and satisfying texture,
Tagliatelle alla Bolognese is often finished with a generous sprinkling of freshly grated Parmesan cheese.
The wide strands of tagliatelle are perfect for holding the thick sauce, ensuring every bite is packed with flavor.
Whether served at a family dinner or on a special occasion, this timeless Italian favorite is loved around the world for its simplicity and delicious taste.

INGREDIENTS

  • 400g tagliatelle pasta
  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 2 carrots, finely chopped
  • 2 celery stalks, finely chopped
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 400g canned chopped tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 cup beef stock
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • Salt to taste
  • Black pepper to taste
  • Freshly grated Parmesan cheese, for serving
  • Fresh parsley (optional), for garnish

HOW TO PREPARE "TAGLIATELLE-ALLA-BOLOGNA"

  1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onions, carrots, and celery, and sauté until softened.
  2. Add the minced garlic and cook for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks.
  4. Stir in the tomato paste, chopped tomatoes, and beef stock. Mix well.
  5. Add salt, pepper, oregano, and basil. Reduce heat and let the sauce simmer gently for 45 minutes to 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
  6. Stir in the milk and allow the sauce to simmer for another 10–15 minutes. This helps make the sauce rich and smooth.
  7. While the sauce is simmering, cook the tagliatelle in salted boiling water according to package instructions until al dente. Drain.
  8. Serve the sauce over the tagliatelle and top with freshly grated Parmesan cheese and parsley if desired.
