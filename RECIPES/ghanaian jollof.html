DELICIOUS GHANAIAN ASSORTED JOLLOF RICE



DESCRIPTION

Assorted Ghanaian jollof rice is a rich, flavorful one-pot dish made with long-grain rice cooked in a well-seasoned tomato-based sauce.
It is “assorted” because it includes a variety of proteins such as beef, chicken, sausages, and sometimes goat meat or shito-seasoned meats,
all cooked together to build deep, smoky layers of flavor. The rice absorbs the spicy blend of tomatoes, onions, peppers, garlic, and traditional Ghanaian spices,
giving it its signature reddish color and bold taste.

What makes Ghanaian jollof special is its slightly smoky aroma and well-balanced heat, often achieved by allowing the rice at the bottom of the pot to lightly toast,
creating a “party jollof” effect. It is commonly served at celebrations, gatherings, and family events, usually paired with fried plantains, salad, or a chilled drink.

INGREDIENTS

  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 500g chicken pieces
  • 300g beef (or goat meat)
  • 2–3 sausages (optional)
  • 4–5 large tomatoes (blended)
  • 2–3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 large onions (one blended, one sliced)
  • 2–3 red bell peppers (blended)
  • 2–3 scotch bonnet peppers (to taste)
  • 4 cloves garlic
  • 1 small piece of ginger
  • 1/3 cup vegetable oil
  • 2–3 cups chicken or beef stock
  • 1–2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 2 bay leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Seasoning cubes (to taste)
  • Optional: carrots, green peas, or mixed vegetables

HOW TO PREPARE "GHANAIAN ASSORTED JOLLOF"

  1. Season the chicken and beef with salt, seasoning cubes, garlic, ginger, onions, and spices. Let it marinate for at least 30 minutes, then boil or grill until partially cooked. Set aside the stock.
  2. Heat vegetable oil in a large pot and fry the cooked meats lightly until golden. Remove and set aside.
  3. In the same oil, sauté chopped onions until soft, then add tomato paste and fry for a few minutes to reduce acidity.
  4. Add the blended mixture of tomatoes, peppers, garlic, and ginger. Cook on medium heat until the sauce thickens and the oil begins to separate from the stew.
  5. Season the stew with curry powder, thyme, bay leaves, salt, and seasoning cubes. Stir well.
  6. Add the meat stock and bring the mixture to a gentle boil.
  7. Pour in the washed rice and stir to combine evenly with the sauce.
  9. Cover and cook on low heat until the rice absorbs the liquid and becomes tender. Stir occasionally to prevent burning.
  10. Add the fried meats back into the pot and mix gently. Allow everything to steam together for a few minutes.
  12. Serve hot with fried plantains, salad, or your preferred side.
