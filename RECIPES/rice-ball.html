DELICIOUS GHANAIAN RICE BALL WITH GROUNDNUT SOUP



DESCRIPTION

Ghanaian rice balls served with groundnut soup is a rich,hearty, and deeply satisfying traditional dish.
The rice balls are made by pounding cooked rice into a smooth, stretchy dough-like form, then shaped into soft,firm balls that are perfect for dipping.
The groundnut soup is creamy and flavorful, prepared from roasted peanuts blended into a thick base and cooked with tomatoes, onions, spices, and palm oil to create a rich, nutty taste. The soup is enhanced with tender pieces of chicken and assorted meat, which absorb the spices and add depth to every bite.
This combination delivers a perfect balance of creaminess, spice, and protein, making it a popular comfort food enjoyed across Ghana,
especially during family gatherings and special occasions.

INGREDIENTS

    For the rice balls

  • 3 cups short-grain or medium-grain rice
  • Water (for boiling and pounding)
  • A pinch of salt (optional)

    • For the groundnut soup

  • 1–2 cups groundnut paste (peanut butter, unsweetened)
  • 500g chicken pieces
  • 300g assorted meat (beef, goat, or lamb)
  • 2–3 large tomatoes (blended)
  • 1 large onion (blended or chopped)
  • 2–3 cloves garlic
  • Small piece of ginger
  • 2–3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2–3 tablespoons palm oil
  • 2–3 cups meat or chicken stock
  • 2–3 scotch bonnet peppers (to taste)
  • Salt to taste
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Optional: okra, garden eggs, or smoked fish for extra flavor

PROCEDURE

  1. Wash the chicken and assorted meat, then season with salt, onion, garlic, ginger, and seasoning cubes. Let it marinate for about 30 minutes.
  2. Cook the meat and chicken in a pot with some water until tender. Reserve the stock for the soup.
  3. In a separate pot, heat palm oil and sauté onions until soft. Add tomato paste and cook for a few minutes.
  4. Add blended tomatoes, peppers, garlic, and ginger. Cook until the mixture thickens and the oil begins to float on top.
  5. Stir in the groundnut paste and gradually add the meat stock while stirring to prevent lumps.
  6. Add the cooked meat and chicken into the soup. Season with salt and seasoning cubes, then simmer on low heat for 20–30 minutes until the soup is rich and well combined.
  7. While the soup is cooking, boil the rice in water until very soft. Drain excess water if needed.
  8. Pound or mash the cooked rice until smooth and stretchy, adding small amounts of water if necessary to achieve a dough-like texture.
  9. Shape the pounded rice into balls using your hands or a spoon.
  10. Serve the rice balls hot with the rich groundnut soup and meat.
